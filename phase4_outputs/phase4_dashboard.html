4. Institutional AI Governance Charter & Human-in-the-Loop Protocol

Mandatory Governance Rules for Educational Institutions:
  1. Strictly Advisory Designation: The AI system provides risk scores solely to trigger personalized supportive counseling and tutoring. It is legally prohibited from being used to deny enrollment, expel students, or automate disciplinary actions.
  2. XAI Review Requirement: Prior to reaching out to any flagged student, counselors must inspect the student's SHAP/LIME factor attribution card to ensure interventions address the root cause (e.g., transportation aid for low attendance vs. peer tutoring for low GPA).
  3. Student Right to Explanation & Appeal: In compliance with data protection laws (LGPD / GDPR), any student has the right to inspect their risk evaluation and request a human review of their academic trajectory.
  4. Semi-Annual Fairness Recalibration: The AI Governance Committee must re-evaluate Disparate Impact and Equal Opportunity every academic semester to safeguard against demographic concept drift.
